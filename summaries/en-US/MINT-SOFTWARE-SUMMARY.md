## You can

- **Distribute** a product containing the Work, for any purpose, including commercially.
- **Keep proprietary modules proprietary**, provided they are not derived from the Work.
- **Choose the notice location** — About screen, documentation, or a license file.

## You must

- **Provide a notice on distribution.** When you distribute a product containing the Work, make available a notice that identifies the MINT modules applied and includes the warranty disclaimer.
- **Respect the modules.** If the product also includes CODE, DESIGN, or DATA components, their obligations apply in parallel.

## You cannot

- **Absorb or replace module obligations.** This module does not replace the file-level reciprocity of CODE, the notice obligation of DESIGN, or the share-alike of DATA.
- **Treat the product as a single-license work.** A product containing multiple MINT components carries multiple, cumulative obligations.

## Scope

This module applies to Distribution of the product as a whole. It does not apply to internal use within an organization.

## No infection

A proprietary module that is not derived from the Work is not subject to MINT solely because it is distributed together with the Work.
